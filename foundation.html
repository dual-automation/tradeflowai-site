Foundation AI™ The Engine Behind Our Industry AI Platforms

Foundation AI™ powers the structured analysis, documentation, proposal logic, and workflow intelligence behind Dual Automation™ products like TradeFlow Electrical AI™, PlansAI™, and ProposalBuilder AI™.

Built for Repeatable Business Workflows

Foundation AI™ is not a standalone chatbot. It is the underlying intelligence framework used to turn messy business information into structured outputs, repeatable workflows, and professional deliverables.

Analyze

Reviews project inputs, documents, descriptions, scope notes, and supporting details.

Structure

Converts raw information into organized assumptions, risks, summaries, and action-ready documentation.

Deploy

Supports expert-managed AI systems that can be trained, refined, and improved over time.
