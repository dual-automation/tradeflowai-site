TradeFlow Electrical AI™ currently supports two core models: one for projects with plans and one for projects without plans.

PlansAI™

For projects with plans

Upload electrical drawings, specifications, addenda, and scope documents. PlansAI™ helps generate a structured bid package for review and submission.

  • ✔ Document intake summary
  • ✔ Bid intelligence report
  • ✔ Customer proposal package
  • ✔ Assumptions and exclusions
  • ✔ Internal pro forma
  • ✔ Draw schedule
  • ✔ Performer / field execution notes

ProposalBuilder AI™

For projects without plans

Expert-Trained Over Time

TradeFlow Electrical AI™ is designed as a managed AI system. As projects are processed, workflows improve, documentation becomes sharper, and the AI knowledge base can be updated to reflect real-world contractor use.

The system gets smarter because it is continuously refined — not because customers are left alone with another dashboard.

Vendor-Neutral Cost Intelligence

TradeFlow Electrical AI™ is designed around standard cost logic, project assumptions, regional review, and contractor approval. It is not tied to City Electric Supply, Graybar, or any single vendor pricing model.

Final pricing should always be reviewed and approved by the contractor before submission.
